How to practise (~15 min a day)
  1. Listen to your clone 3×, once at 0.75×. Watch the blue line: where it rises, falls, pauses.
  2. Echo: listen, then say it right after.
  3. Shadow (headphones): speak along with it, a split second behind.
  4. Compare: A/B play, look at the two lines, read the numbers.
  5. Repeat until you can't hear the difference in A/B. Then mark "Nailed it".

The numbers are rough acoustic indicators, not a grade. Your ear in A/B is the real judge.

Why your own voice helps

Imitating a model of your own voice lowers memory load and makes it easier to hear exactly what differs, because timbre is no longer a distraction. Shadowing combined with reading aloud has improved rhythm, intonation and sentence-final lengthening in controlled studies. Gains tend to be specific to what you drill, and they depend on steady, motivated practice, so short daily sessions beat occasional long ones.
